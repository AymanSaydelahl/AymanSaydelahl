## English Profile

### Executive Summary
Senior executive and strategic advisor with approximately 33 years of service in the Egyptian Ministry of Interior, holding leadership roles including Deputy Director of Cairo Civil Protection and General Director of Explosives & Counter-Terrorism, alongside securing VIPs and major events in Cairo, leading firefighting and rescue teams, and building emergency and facility protection plans aligned with Egyptian codes. Achieved a 50% reduction in emergency response times in Cairo through optimized smart distribution of response forces.

Holds a Bachelor's degree from the Police Academy in Egypt (1991), a Bachelor of Science in Journalism from Zagazig University (1986–1990), and a Master's degree, with advanced executive and security training in the United Kingdom, the United States, Louisiana State University, and Cranfield (France).

Currently leading the strategic restructuring and expansion of **PX4D** toward a global consulting and digital education model across:
* **Official Website:** https://px4d.com
* **Training & Content Portals:** `t.px4d.com` | `tadreeb.px4d.com`
* **Store & Strategic Policy Papers:** https://store.px4d.com

Advocates for establishing a **National Safety Authority** in Egypt and other countries lacking such an entity to embed safety awareness from primary education through university level.

### Registered Methodologies & Frameworks (SSRN / Elsevier)
* **HRDA (High Risk Decision Architecture):** Designed to overcome cognitive and neurological freezing within the first 60 seconds of an acute crisis, addressing amygdala impact that severs executive signals to the limbs.
* **ISVM (Integrated Security Vision Matrix):** Framework for proactive gap detection and institutional alignment.
* **A-ORCA:** Methodology for managing complex operations, crisis opportunity management, and adaptive resilience.

### Selected Training & Advisory Track Record
* Designed and delivered executive crisis management, emergency response, and occupational safety training and simulation programs for organizations including **Emirates Nuclear Energy Corporation (ENEC)**, **Dubai Civil Defense Academy** (via Golden Vision Training & Consultancy), and private-sector leaders such as **Emaar**.
* Built custom training curricula and delivered programs to over 500 employees, contributing to a 50% reduction in workplace incidents.
* Regular security, crisis, and safety media commentator on **Al-Hadath Al-Youm**, **Sada El-Balad**, and **Al-Shams**.

### المنهجيات العلمية والأطر الاستراتيجية المسجلة (SSRN / Elsevier)
* **HRDA (High Risk Decision Architecture):** هندسة اتخاذ القرار عالي المخاطر لتجاوز التجمّد الذهني والعصبي في أول 60 ثانية من الأزمة، وتفادي تأثير اللوزة الدماغية الذي يقطع التواصل ويؤثر على وصول الإشارات التنفيذية إلى الأطراف.
* **ISVM (Integrated Security Vision Matrix — مصفوفة الرؤية الأمنية الشاملة):** إطار لاكتشاف الفجوات الاستباقية وتحقيق المواءمة المؤسسية.
* **A-ORCA:** منهجية إدارة العمليات المعقدة وعلم إدارة الفرص وقت الأزمة وبناء المرونة التكيفية.
